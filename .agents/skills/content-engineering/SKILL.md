# Content Engineering Development Skill

## Purpose
Guides the Content Engineering role in building the Viral Content Research Agent and Creative Scriptwriting Agent.

## Core Rules
1. **Fact-Checking Gate**: Never approve a topic if the central claim cannot be verified through credible sources (academic journals, space agencies, historical archives). Never fabricate sources.
2. **Scoring Formula**:
   - Curiosity & Hook Potential: 25%
   - Surprise & Novelty: 20%
   - Visual Storytelling Potential: 20%
   - Audience Relevance: 15%
   - Narrative Depth: 10%
   - Freshness & Originality: 10%
3. **80-Second Structure**:
   - 0–4s: Immediate visceral hook (zero greetings or filler).
   - 4–14s: Mystery / central question.
   - 14–29s: First revelation.
   - 29–50s: Escalation & explanation.
   - 50–66s: Climax / breakthrough.
   - 66–80s: Complete payoff.
4. **Pacing Target**: 165–185 words, calibrated to speech duration.
